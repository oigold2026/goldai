1. Privacy

Kanye Series may collect information needed to provide the store, including account details, contact information, delivery information, cart and wishlist data, order information, and information you submit when requesting a special order.

Information is used to operate the service, process orders, communicate about orders, provide account features, improve the website, and help prevent fraud or misuse. Payment processing may also involve information being exchanged with the payment provider you choose.

Do not submit passwords, payment-card details, or other highly sensitive information into product, special-order, or general-purpose message fields.

2. Orders & delivery

Please review products, quantities, delivery details, phone numbers, and totals before placing an order. Order status may change as the order is processed.

Delivery timing can depend on product availability, location, courier availability, weekends, public holidays, and other circumstances outside the website's control. We may contact you using the information supplied with the order when clarification is required.

3. Payments & refunds

Where PesaPal or another displayed payment method is used, payment status is subject to verification by the payment provider and Kanye Series. A browser return to the website does not by itself mean that payment has been completed.

If a payment is pending, failed, reversed, duplicated, or otherwise disputed, the order may remain pending while the payment is checked. Refunds, where applicable, are handled according to the order circumstances and the applicable payment-provider process.

Never send payment-card PINs, passwords, or security codes to Kanye Series through chat or order notes.

4. Accounts & security

You are responsible for keeping your account credentials private and for notifying us if you believe your account has been accessed without permission. Account information should be accurate and kept up to date.

Administrative functions are restricted to authorized administrators. Attempts to bypass access controls, manipulate prices or order records, or interfere with the service are not permitted.

5. Returns & cancellations

Return, replacement, and cancellation eligibility may depend on the product, order status, stock availability, product condition, and the reason for the request. Contact Kanye Series as soon as possible if there is a problem with an order.

Special-order items may have different cancellation or return conditions because they can be sourced or prepared specifically for a customer. Those conditions should be confirmed before a special order is accepted.

6. Cookies & local storage

The website may use browser storage and similar technologies for essential functionality, preferences, authentication-related state, cart experiences, and the PWA experience. Your browser may allow you to control or clear stored data, although doing so can affect functionality.

7. Website availability

We aim to keep Kanye Series available and accurate, but the service may occasionally be unavailable because of maintenance, connectivity problems, third-party service outages, or other technical issues.

8. Policy changes

These policies may be updated when the store, payment methods, delivery process, or applicable requirements change. The latest version published on this page applies going forward.

Questions or an order problem?

Use the contact details shown on the Kanye Series website or in your order communication. Keep your order ID available when asking about an order.
